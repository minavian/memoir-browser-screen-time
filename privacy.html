Memoir

Privacy policy

Everything stays on your computer.

Memoir has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no ads. It never sends your data off your computer, so nobody else can see it, including the developer.

Effective 19 October 2026

What Memoir records

To show where your browsing time goes, Memoir keeps these in your browser:

  • Which website you’re on, and for how long. It keeps the site’s name (for example youtube.com), the time you spent there and the hours of the day it was in. It does not keep the addresses of the pages you visit, unless you turn on Page titles. Tabs that aren’t websites are counted under a general name only: Memoir, New Tab, Browser pages, Extensions or Files.
  • Page titles and their addresses, only if you turn on “Page titles” in Settings (it’s off unless you turn it on). They are kept as long as the rest of your history, and deleted with it.
  • Each site’s home address (for example https://www.youtube.com), so its icon and link work.
  • Your settings: your daily limit and when it changed, your reminder day and time, categories you changed, sites you never track, and your Appearance choices (the theme, the Rainbow clock and the cursor).

To know when to stop counting, Memoir asks Chrome whether your computer is active, idle or locked. It only learns that state, never what you type or click.

What Memoir never records: what you type or click, what’s on the pages, passwords, form entries, cookies or your location.

Time on sites on your Never track list counts only towards one combined “Private” total, with no site names, titles or addresses. If you let Memoir run in private (Incognito) windows, their time is counted the same way.

Where it’s kept

  • On your computer only, in Chrome’s storage for the extension. It is not synced to your Google account or your other devices.
  • Memoir makes no network requests. Site icons come from Chrome’s own icon cache, so the sites themselves aren’t contacted.
  • Notifications (your daily limit, the weekly reminder) are made on your computer.

Linking with Memoir for Windows

Memoir can sync with Memoir for Windows, the desktop app, on the same computer, so both show the same week. It’s off until you press Link in Settings, under Your data, and your browser asks your permission first.

  • On this computer only. The two talk through your browser’s native messaging, which starts Memoir for Windows’ own program on your computer, and a named pipe that only your Windows account can open. Nothing goes online, and there is no server in between.
  • What goes across: your daily totals (each site’s and app’s time, hour by hour), with page titles and their addresses only if Page titles is on; your rules (Never track, Page titles, Keep history, the weekly reminder, your daily limit and its history, categories you changed, whether tracking is paused, and which limit warnings you’ve had today); each site’s home address; your Appearance choices; your Windows apps’ names, categories and icons, to this browser; the icons of the sites you visit, from Chrome’s own icon cache, to Memoir for Windows; and a note when you use Clear all or erase saved page titles, so the other side does the same.
  • The tab in front. While linked, Memoir tells Memoir for Windows which tab is in front, so both count the same time: its site, and its title, which the app keeps only if Page titles is on. A tab in a private (Incognito) window counts only as Private.
  • Kept apart. Each side keeps the other’s days apart from its own, and shows the two merged, so the same minutes never count twice. When you link, you choose to merge both histories or to start from today.
  • More than one browser. If you link Memoir in several browsers or profiles, Memoir for Windows passes each one’s time on to the others.
  • Unlinking, in either one’s Settings, stops it, and both keep what they have. While linked, Clear all on either side clears both.

How it’s used

Your data is used only to show you your own time: in the popup, the weekly report, the limit warnings and the weekly reminder, and in Memoir for Windows if you link the two.

It is never sold or shared. It is never used for advertising, and never used to decide creditworthiness or for lending. The developer never receives it.

Your choices

  • Pause tracking at any time, with the Track browsing switch in Settings.
  • Never track particular sites.
  • Choose how long to keep history: forever, 1 year, 6 months or 3 months. Older days are deleted automatically, page titles included.
  • Link or unlink Memoir for Windows, in Settings under Your data. It’s off unless you link it.
  • Export everything as a file, or import a file you exported earlier. The file goes wherever you save it; Memoir doesn’t upload it.
  • Clear all deletes everything at once. Removing the extension deletes everything it keeps too; if it was linked, Memoir for Windows keeps its own copy until you clear it there.

Links and email

  • Links in the report, to a site or a page, open only when you click them. Those sites’ own privacy policies then apply.
  • The “bug report” link in Settings opens your own email app with a new message. The message’s subject is Memoir’s version number and nothing else is filled in. Nothing is sent until you send it yourself.

Updates

New versions of Memoir come through the Chrome Web Store. Google Chrome checks for them and installs them itself, the same way it updates all your extensions; that check is Chrome’s own, under Google’s privacy policy. Memoir doesn’t download anything or update itself, and none of your Memoir data is sent along. Your records and settings stay as they are after an update.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with a new date. If Memoir ever needed to send any data off your computer, it would ask you first.
