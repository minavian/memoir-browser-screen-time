Memoir

How Memoir works

Only the time you spend looking counts.

Memoir times the tab in front of you, and stops when you look away. Here’s exactly what counts, when the clock stops, and what’s kept.

What counts

  • The tab you’re looking at, in the browser window that’s in front. Other tabs, and windows behind other apps, don’t count, even if a video is playing in them.
  • Every kind of tab. Websites count under their name, like youtube.com. Tabs that aren’t websites count under one name each: Memoir itself, New Tab, Browser pages (Settings, History, Downloads and the like), Extensions and Files you open from your computer.
  • By the hour. Each day keeps how long you spent on each site, and in which hours, so the report can show your day hour by hour.

When the clock stops

  • Another app is in front, or the browser is minimised.
  • You haven’t touched the mouse or keyboard for 60 seconds. If the tab is playing sound, like a video or music, it keeps counting.
  • The screen locks, or the computer goes to sleep. After a long silence, at most a minute is counted.
  • You pause it with Track browsing in Settings.

Private time

Sites on your Never track list still count towards your total and your limit, and among the categories as Other, but with no names: a day or an hour shows it as one Private line. Adding a site makes its past time Private too. Private (Incognito) windows count the same way, if you let Memoir run in them.

What’s kept

  • Each site’s name and its time, hour by hour. Never what you type, click or read.
  • Page titles and their links, only if you turn them on in Settings: every page each day, and after a week a site’s 30 with the most time, the rest as “other pages”.
  • Everything is kept for as long as you choose under Keep history (forever, unless you pick a shorter time).
  • It all stays on your computer: in this browser, and in Memoir for Windows if you link the two. More in the privacy policy.

Linked with Memoir for Windows

  • Optional. Press Link in Settings, under Your data, and this browser and Memoir for Windows show the same week. You choose to merge both histories, or to start from today.
  • Both keep counting. Each keeps the other’s days apart from its own and shows the two merged: where both counted the same hour, the larger time counts, never the two added together.
  • Apps and Browsers. Memoir for Windows counts your browser as an app, with all its time, and Memoir here counts the sites in it: the same minutes, seen two ways. Most used shows them as two lists, Apps and Browsers; your totals, your limit, your categories and your hours count each minute once.
  • Your Windows apps show in the report too, by name, icon and category, and Memoir for Windows shows your sites with their own icons.
  • One set of rules. Never track, Page titles, Keep history, the weekly reminder, your daily limit, categories, pausing and Appearance (the theme, the cursor and the Rainbow clock) follow whichever side changed them last.
  • While Memoir for Windows is open, it sends the limit and weekly notifications, so each comes once.
  • A newer Memoir for Windows. This extension knows the newest one, and tells a linked Memoir for Windows; an older one then says so in its Settings, with a link to Memoir’s website. Neither looks online.
  • Unlink from either side and both keep what they have. While linked, Clear all on either side clears both.
  • It runs through your browser’s native messaging and a named pipe only your Windows account can open: on this computer, never online.

Your daily limit

  • The ring is green, then yellow at 75% of your limit, orange at 90% and red at 95%, when you get a notification. Past the limit it turns deep red with a flame, and you’re notified once more.
  • Once you commit to a limit, it’s locked: it stays the same on the days after. Only Settings can undo it, after a run of questions.

Light on your browser

  • Nothing runs inside the pages you visit. Memoir asks the browser which tab is in front, so it never slows a website down or sees what’s on it.
  • Its background part works when you switch tabs or windows, and saves once a minute. The report and the toolbar popup run only while they’re open.

Closing the report

Pull the gear at the top right down and let go to close the report’s tab. Leave the mouse by the gear for a moment and it shows you how. A click still opens Settings.

Why it may differ from other screen-time tools

Your phone’s or your computer’s own screen time counts every app, and any time the screen is on. Memoir counts only the browser, and only while you’re using it. So its numbers are usually smaller, and they tell you where your browsing went.

The clock is real

Memoir’s clock tells the time: the dot goes round the ring once a minute, the long hand shows the minutes and the short hand the hours, on your computer’s own time. Like the one at the top of this page.
